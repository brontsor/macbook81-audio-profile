# macbook81-audio-profile

Speaker names and the equalizer for the MacBook (Retina, 12-inch, Early 2015), model `MacBook8,1`, on Omarchy. Not the driver.

- Userspace only. Run as the desktop user, not root.
- Do not copy these files into `macbook81-cs4208`.
- Do not restart WirePlumber from the apply script.
- The coefficients are layout100 from Thomas Shirley's `51-macbook81-speaker.conf`. Not measured here. Not A/B'd. Do not call them tuned.
- Do not push, add a remote, or change visibility unless asked.
- Do not write a session recap into this repository.
- Do not record a serial, a manufacture week, or a battery date.
